# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-24

- Show crafting costs directly in the WoW Forever crafting window using Goldwright minimum prices and market value.
- Compare profit at minimum and market selling prices for both crafting costs, after the auction house fee.
- Automatically learn vendor purchase prices and use them for crafting materials.
- Save vendor prices per character and update crafting costs immediately when visiting a vendor.
- Display positive profits in green, negative profits in red, and Unknown when required prices are missing.
