# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The tooltip shown when hovering over the table now includes a per-ingredient breakdown of crafting costs.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-26

- Add scrolling to keep crafting costs accessible when recipe materials fill the crafting window.
- Show a compact crafting cost display beside the enchant target slot, while retaining the full profit table for craftable items within Enchanting.
- Identify Bind on Pickup outputs with a “Cannot be auctioned (BoP)” heading and hide their profit columns while keeping crafting costs visible.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-24

- Show crafting costs directly in the WoW Forever crafting window using Goldwright minimum prices and market value.
- Compare profit at minimum and market selling prices for both crafting costs, after the auction house fee.
- Automatically learn vendor purchase prices and use them for crafting materials.
- Save vendor prices per character and update crafting costs immediately when visiting a vendor.
- Display positive profits in green, negative profits in red, and Unknown when required prices are missing.
